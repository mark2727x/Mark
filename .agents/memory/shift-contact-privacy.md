---

## Rule
Require a phone number for new accounts, require an exact address and future ISO start time for shifts, and expose phone contacts only to the manager and lifeguard assigned to a filled shift.

**Why:** Phone numbers are sensitive personal data, while accurate location and schedule data are necessary for a safe shift handoff.

**How to apply:** Keep public profiles and open-shift responses free of phone numbers. Authenticated users may see their own number, and filled-shift detail responses may include only the other assigned party's contact.
